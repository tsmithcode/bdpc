Recognize the problem

Payroll trouble rarely starts with one dramatic failure.

It usually starts with missing information, unclear ownership, inconsistent review, or an overloaded operator. These are the signals that outside support may be appropriate.

01

Employees question their pay

Wrong dollar amounts, deductions, taxes, benefits, garnishments, or off-cycle adjustments create urgency and erode trust.

02

The owner is overwhelmed

Payroll competes with sales, delivery, hiring, cash flow, and client service—while every deadline still matters.

03

Records do not reconcile

Current payroll, year-to-date totals, prior returns, and platform reports do not tell the same story.

04

A key person leaves

A resignation, leave, backlog, or acquisition reveals that critical payroll knowledge was concentrated in one person.

05

A system is changing

Setup, conversion, data mapping, testing, first payroll, and documentation require focused control.

06

Approvals arrive too late

Missing records, slow decisions, unclear authority, or insufficient funding can stop a responsible payroll release.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before confidential data changes hands.

What business size is a fit?

M Kathleen is initially optimized for small and growing organizations and bounded projects. A larger company can still be a fit when the assignment is defined, access is controlled, and authority is clear. Capacity is scoped—not guessed solely from employee count.

Which payroll schedules are supported?

Weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, monthly, quarterly, and off-cycle work can be considered. The written scope confirms cutoffs, approval windows, banking requirements, and responsibilities.

Will M Kathleen take over all employer responsibility?

No. The employer remains responsible for accurate source data, worker classification, funding, approvals, required registrations, monitoring deposits and notices, and legal compliance. The engagement defines M Kathleen’s operational role.

Can you fix a payroll emergency?

Late deposits, incorrect checks, failed software, missing funding, or incomplete records may be emergencies, but resolution depends on access, authority, platform availability, bank timing, and third parties. Emergency availability and outcomes are never guaranteed.

How is confidential information handled?

Do not send Social Security numbers, bank details, employee files, passwords, medical information, or full payroll exports through a public form or first-contact email. After fit and scope are confirmed, a secure transfer method and access controls must be agreed.

Is M Kathleen a law firm, CPA firm, or tax agency?

No. Payroll operations support is not legal advice, tax advice, financial-statement audit, benefits brokerage, worker-classification counsel, or agency representation. Appropriate specialists are engaged or referred when needed.
