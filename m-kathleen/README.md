- Live site: https://tsmithcode.github.io/bdpc/m-kathleen/
- Stack: semantic HTML, modern CSS, minimal vanilla JavaScript, original SVG social media artwork
- Primary market: payroll operations support for Metro Atlanta and remote clients
- Launch posture: payroll-first; residential real estate remains explicitly in development

Each service communicates the pain case, deliverables, client responsibilities, commercial range, and professional boundary using progressive disclosure.

## Evidence and trust controls

- Career facts are labeled as prior career experience—not M Kathleen client results.
- No fabricated or synthetic testimonials are published.
- Public prices are planning ranges, not binding proposals.
- Sensitive payroll information must not be sent through first-contact email.
- Operational payroll support is not legal, tax, accounting, benefits-brokerage, investment, or HR legal advice.
- Real estate services are not advertised until licensing, brokerage affiliation, and required disclosures are confirmed.
- References to OpenAI, Y Combinator, ADP, Gusto, and Justworks identify public communication patterns only and do not imply endorsement or partnership.
