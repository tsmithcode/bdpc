Agency standard

Own the outcome without overstepping authority

Do

  • Bring a recommended solution with every blocker.
  • Translate discussion into owner, deliverable, evidence, and next check.
  • Use the structural grid, coordinated sheets, and visible design intent as the organizing truth.
  • State uncertainty early and bound it.
  • Confirm before acting outside written scope.

Avoid

  • Long biographies, tool explanations, or automation demonstrations during operational alignment.
  • Reporting effort without a reviewable artifact.
  • Multiple open-ended questions when one bounded decision will move the work.
  • Calling progressive work complete.
  • Treating verbal discussion as professional issue authority or commercial authorization.
